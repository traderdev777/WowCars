Understanding Spare Part Types

We maintain strict transparency: we never mislabel aftermarket parts as genuine OEM.

Genuine / OEM

Original Equipment Manufacturer

Manufactured specifically for the carmaker under direct branding (Maruti Genuine Parts, Hyundai Mobis, Tata Motors Genuine Parts).

Browse OEM Parts →
OES Quality

Original Equipment Supplier

Produced by Tier-1 suppliers who supply to the factory assembly lines (Bosch, Denso, Valeo, Brembo, Sachs, Mann-Filter) in their own branding.

Browse OES Parts →
Aftermarket

Certified Replacement Parts

Cost-effective replacement parts meeting or exceeding automotive safety regulations, ideal for older or out-of-warranty models.
